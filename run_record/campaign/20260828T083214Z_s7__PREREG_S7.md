## 0. What A22 is, in the entry's own arithmetic

`tools/target_model.py` v4 carries one additive design term on top of the three-level
decomposition:

    ate(a, o) = g + LAM_BTW*(m(o) - g) + KAPPA*S_MULT*s(a)*L(o) + A_MULT*ASSERTION_MATCH[a, o]

with `A_MULT = 0.40` and

| cell | raw | **× A_MULT = pp of scale range added** |
|---|---|---|
| Funding × funding_perceptions | 2.5 | **1.00** |
| Consensus × belief_post | 1.8 | **0.72** |
| High public trust × trust_post | 0.9 | **0.36** |
| High public trust × trust_multidimensional | 0.6 | **0.24** |

These four cells are the entry's two largest ATEs and two of its top five. The whole term
rests on an argument (DESIGN §6.6, §11.7), not on a measurement. A22 buys the measurement.

**The design fact the measurement is about** (read off `/workspace/benchmark/survey/questionnaire.txt`
this session, public instrument, no outcome data): three of the sixteen arms have an explicit
*elicit-and-correct* structure.

* **Funding** elicits agreement with "The federal government allocates significant resources to
  climate change research" and with "Climate scientists receive large amounts of private research
  funding", then corrects each on-screen with dollar figures ($10.6bn vs $52.5bn; $7bn vs $160bn).
* **Consensus** elicits three percentage estimates on 0–100 sliders and gives per-item feedback
  with the correct numbers (99%, ~100%, 66%).
* **High public trust** elicits "What percentage of Americans trust climate scientists…" on a
  0–100 slider and corrects it with 76% from Pew.

**And the fact that decides which estimand matters**: *no scored outcome of the target study
asks for a corrected quantity back.* `funding_perceptions` is `funding_5`, "is the federal
government spending too much / too little on climate change research" (a normative judgment
about the corrected quantity); `belief_post` asks the respondent's own accuracy rating of
"Human activities are causing climate change" (not the % of scientists who agree);
`trust_post` / `trust_multidimensional` ask the respondent's own trust (not the % of Americans
who trust). The entry has **zero** cells of the "same quantity re-asked" kind.

## 1. The estimand, fixed before the answer

For a train arm that (i) elicits a quantity from the respondent and (ii) corrects it on-screen
with a specific number, define, in **pp of the outcome's scale range**:

* **E_arm** = ATE(correcting arm) − mean ATE(the same study's other, non-correcting message
  arms) on the corrected item. Requires ≥ 2 message arms.
* **E_out** = ATE(correcting arm) on the corrected item − ATE(the same arm) on the study's
  comparable non-corrected outcomes. Works in a 2-arm study.

Both estimate the same thing — the entry's assertion term is a single-cell bump, so it shows
up in the arm contrast and in the outcome contrast alike. The raw **E_abs** = ATE vs control on
the corrected item is reported too, as a bound, but it is *not* the estimand: the entry already
predicts a base effect on every cell, and the assertion term is the excess on top of it.

**Item classes, fixed in advance:**

| class | definition | entry cells |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | the scored item asks for the **same quantity** that was corrected (e.g. a consensus-estimate slider after a "99%" correction) | **none** |
| **C2** | the scored item asks the respondent's **own belief/attitude about the claim** whose evidence was corrected | Funding × funding_perceptions (1.00), Consensus × belief_post (0.72) |
| **C2n** | as C2, but the corrected quantity is a **social norm** (what other people think), and the item asks the respondent's own attitude | High public trust × trust_post (0.36), × trust_multidimensional (0.24) |
| **C3** | any other outcome of a correcting arm | none |

**The deciding number is Ê(C2)**: the precision-weighted pooled excess over the C2 class,
with its standard error and its degrees of freedom (R29: no mechanism claim on < 3 dof).
The entry's implied C2 value is the mean of its two full cells, **0.86 pp**.

## 2. Decision rules — what each answer does

Let `Ê` and `se` be the pooled C2 excess and its standard error as the child reports them.

**Governance first (R27).** `A_MULT`'s warrant is *not* a validation measurement: v2 chose 0.40
as the smallest value at which the assertion cell is still the Funding arm's own largest cell,
subject to the mix band R20 takes from the validation environment. The *value* is authored; the
*constraint* is validation-sourced. So a train measurement of the same quantity may move
`A_MULT` under the split rule, **provided the resulting table stays inside the validated mix
band [0.15, 0.30]**. Computed this session, before the answer (model-side mix = sd_wth/sd_btw
over the 16 × 13 intervention cells):

| A_MULT | 0.00 | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.30 | **0.40** | 0.50 | 0.60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mix | 0.163 | 0.165 | 0.181 | 0.206 | **0.238** | 0.274 | 0.313 |

Every **downward** move is admissible; the band binds only above **A_MULT ≈ 0.55**. That
asymmetry is a fact about the entry, not a preference, and it is recorded before the answer is
known.

**Rule A22-1 — the update.** Treat the authored 0.86 pp as a prior with sd **0.40 pp** (the two
authored full cells are 0.72 and 1.00; the authoring is an argument, so it gets a generous sd)
and the measurement as `N(Ê, se²)`. The posterior mean is

    P = (0.86/0.40² + Ê/se²) / (1/0.40² + 1/se²)
    A_MULT_new = clip( round(0.40 × P / 0.86, 2), 0.05, 0.55 )

`ASSERTION_MATCH`'s shape (2.5 / 1.8 / 0.9 / 0.6) is **not** re-authored; only the multiplier
moves. A weak measurement moves the entry little by construction, which is the whole point of
writing the rule before seeing it.

**Rule A22-2 — the dead band.** If `|A_MULT_new − 0.40| < 0.05`, **nothing changes** and v4
stands. In particular: **any Ê in roughly [0.55, 1.25] with a typical se changes nothing**, and
this is the outcome I expect (§3).

**Rule A22-3 — dropping the term.** `A_MULT = 0` only if the child's own 95% upper limit on
Ê(C2) is **< 0.10 pp**. Otherwise the floor is 0.05.

**Rule A22-4 — the ceiling.** No cell may exceed **3 pp** (ANCHORS_D's realistic-message
bound), and `A_MULT ≤ 0.55` (R20). If the measurement argues for more, the entry takes the cap
and the excess is recorded in `OPEN.md` as an unadopted disagreement.

**Rule A22-5 — what explicitly changes nothing.**

1. **Any C1 number.** A +15 pp effect of a consensus correction on a consensus-estimate slider
   is the most likely large number the child will find, and it is worth **zero** to this entry:
   the target scores no C1 item. If the child reports only C1, v4 stands and A22 stays open.
2. **E_abs without a within-study contrast.** A raw 2-arm ATE on the corrected item confounds
   the assertion term with the study's own base effect; only E_arm/E_out enter Ê.
3. **A pre→post change inside the treated arm** that is not differenced against the control
   arm's own pre→post change.
4. **Fewer than 3 dof (R29)**, or a single study standing alone: the pooled Ê must rest on
   ≥ 2 studies and ≥ 3 dof, else the dead band applies whatever the point estimate.
5. **The half-strength coding of the C2n cells** (0.9 / 0.6 against 2.5 / 1.8) changes only if
   the child delivers a **within-study** factual-correction vs norm-correction contrast on
   comparable items whose ratio's 95% CI excludes [0.25, 0.85]. I do not expect this to exist;
   if it does not, the coding stands as authored.
6. **Anything the child reports about targeting, arm ordering, `KAPPA`, `LAM_BTW`, control
   levels, moderation, or donation.** A22 buys one number. Other findings go to `OPEN.md`.

**Rule A22-6 — if A22-1 fires**, v5 is built through the full pipeline (`tools/target_entry.py
--exact --n-control 6000 --n-intervention 3000`, verify, `make manifest && make check`), the
constant keeps its evidence *and its kind* in place (R27), and the report's declaration is made
about **v5**. If it does not fire, the declaration is made about v4. Either way the declaration
is unconditional this session: no third state exists.

## 3. Pre-registered expectation (so the answer can embarrass me)

I expect **Ê(C2) ≈ 0.6–1.2 pp**, i.e. the dead band, for one reason and against one worry.
The reason: the gateway-belief literature's own structure — a large first-stage move on the
corrected quantity, a small second-stage move on the respondent's own belief. The worry:
`funding_perceptions` is one inferential step further from its correction than `belief_post` is
(a normative "too much / too little" judgment about a corrected magnitude), so if the classes
split, I expect the Funding cell to be the weaker of the two and the entry's ordering
(2.5 > 1.8) to be **wrong in shape** even if right in scale. Under A22-1 the shape does not
move; that mis-ordering, if measured, is logged in `OPEN.md` as a new open item rather than
patched, because the rule was written first.

If Ê comes back near **0.2 pp**, `A_MULT` goes to about **0.12**, the entry's two largest cells
shrink by a factor of three, and v5 is built. That is exactly the contingency s6's declaration
named.
