## 4. New standing rules declared here

**R26. Bound a mechanism read by the correlation between the two tables before reading it.**
For prediction tables with corr = rho, the systematic part of any difference in r is at most
about `(1-rho)*|r| + sqrt(1-rho^2)`. If the observed difference is near that ceiling, the
change could not have produced it and the reading is draw noise. Compute rho first.

**R27. Gate governance follows the evidence base, not the file.** An entry constant whose
warrant is the validation environment is governed by the gate and reverts on a REJECTED. An
entry constant whose warrant is train ground truth is governed by the split rule and does not
need a gate. State which kind every constant is, in place.

**R28. Read a matched-amplitude allocation curve for saturation before extrapolating it.**
Two endpoints plus the incumbent are three points; fit the shape, do not assume linearity.
A gain measured against an allocation you would never ship is not a gain over the one you do.
