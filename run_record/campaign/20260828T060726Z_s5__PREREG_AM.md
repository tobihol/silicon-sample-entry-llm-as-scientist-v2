## 0. Why this question, and what a number here buys

Target entry v2 predicts `ate(a,o) = g + LAM_BTW*(m(o)-g) + KAPPA*s(a)*L(o) + A_MULT*A(a,o)`.
`A` is the **assertion-match** term: four cells where the intervention explicitly states a
fact about the quantity the item asks for (Funding x funding_perceptions, Consensus x
belief_post, High-public-trust x trust_post and x trust_multidimensional). `A_MULT = 0.40`
was set in s4 by a *consistency argument* — the smallest multiplier at which the
assertion-match cell is still the Funding arm's own largest cell, which also happens to land
the table's mix inside the validated band. It has never been measured. It carries roughly a
quarter of the entry's predicted variance. REPORT s4 §6 named it the entry's largest
untested bet.

The evidence that motivated it is a **between-study contrast**: `r_within_adj` = 0.784 over
7 draws on kim2024 (a consensus message scored partly on a consensus item) against 0.092 on
beall2017 and 0.112 on goldwert2026. That contrast confounds "assertion match" with
everything else that differs between those studies. **This experiment moves the contrast
inside each study**, where the confound cannot follow.

## 1. The construct, fixed here

A cell `(a,o)` is **content-targeted** iff *the arm's own stimulus states, elicits, or enacts
the specific quantity the outcome item asks about*, so that a respondent could answer the
item partly by reporting the content of the message rather than by inferring from it. Every
other cell is **generic**: its prediction rests on a message-quality ordering
(some arms are better messages than others) rather than on content-to-item matching.

The entry's `A` term is the content-targeted class; `KAPPA*s(a)*L(o)` is the generic class.
The question is **where a fixed message-level variance budget buys more correlation**.

## 2. The design

For each task let `P(o)` be the per-outcome mean of the arm's standing (promoted-method)
prediction and `R = table - P` its message-level residual. The promoted table is
`P + kappa*R` with `kappa` the regime constant (0.20 many-variant, 1.00 distinct); its
message-level norm is `w = kappa*||R||`. Split the cells into `M` (content-targeted) and
`N` (generic) by the fixed rule of §3, and define

* **submission_1 = TARGETED**: `P + (w/||R_M||) * R_M`   (all message variance on M)
* **submission_2 = GENERIC** : `P + (w/||R_N||) * R_N`   (all message variance on N)

Both tables have (i) the **identical outcome profile** `P`, (ii) the **identical total
message-level norm** `w`, hence (iii) the identical total predicted variance and the
identical mix `sd_wth/sd_btw`. `R_M` and `R_N` are each still exactly within-outcome
centred (`M` is a union of whole outcomes wherever this design is used), so
`cov(P, q) = 0` exactly and the two submissions differ **only in where inside the message
level the bet is placed**. This is *not* a re-weighting between the outcome level and the
message level; R18 is not engaged, and nothing here is offered for promotion.

Algebra that makes the read interpretable. With `t` the human half, `D = n*sd_p*sd_t`
(matched across the pair), and `c_j = corr_j * ||t_j||` the covariance a class can deliver
per unit of prediction norm,

    r_adj(TARGETED) = K + X,   X = w*c_M/D
    r_adj(GENERIC)  = K + Y,   Y = w*c_N/D
    r_adj(promoted) = K + alpha*X + beta*Y,   alpha = ||R_M||/||R||, beta = ||R_N||/||R||

`K` (the outcome-level contribution) is common. The r_adj-optimal allocation of the message
budget is `s_M/s_N = c_M/c_N = X/Y`, and my current allocation is `alpha/beta`.

## 4. The instrument, re-calibrated — a correction to REPORT s4 §1

`r_adj` is invariant under any positive **affine** transform of the whole table
(`p -> c*p + d`, c > 0), not merely under a rescale: damping toward the study grand mean is
affine, so those submissions are replications too. Grouping all 50 scored submissions into
affine-equivalence classes by `corr(p_i, p_j) > 0.999999` and pooling the within-class
variance gives

| task | classes used | dof | **sd(r_adj)** | sd of a difference | 3σ threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| beall2017 | 2 | 2 | **0.0116** | 0.0163 | 0.049 |
| dablander2025 | 1 | 6 | **0.0135** | 0.0190 | 0.057 |
| goldwert2026 | 2 | 2 | **0.0455** | 0.0644 | 0.193 |
| kim2024 | 1 | 6 | **0.0817** | 0.1155 | 0.347 |
| altenmueller2024 | 0 | 0 | — (r_adj saturates at 1.000) | | |
| kerwer2025 / orchinik2024 | — | — | reliability ≈ 0, no usable read | | |

**This supersedes REPORT s4 §1's table (beall 0.005, goldwert 0.002).** s4 pooled only the
*promoted-mix* class on those two tasks — one pair each — and missed the second affine class
(s1/1 vs s1/2), which differs by 0.022 on beall and **0.091** on goldwert. The honest pooled
numbers are 2x (beall) and 20x (goldwert) larger. Consequences recorded here in advance of
this run's reads:

* s4's mix result is **not** 17σ / 28σ. It is beall −0.086 = **5.3σ** and goldwert +0.054 =
  **0.8σ**. The beall direction (a higher message/outcome mix hurts) survives; the goldwert
  counter-sign does **not** survive its own noise. The correct statement is "raising the mix
  hurt on the one task that can measure it and was a wash on the other", not "opposite in
  sign". The *decision* is unchanged — `LAM_BTW = 1.00` is the low-mix, beall-favoured
  setting — but X2/R18's evidence is now one significant negative plus one null, and OPEN
  A13 must be re-read in that light.
* **R17 is re-stated with these numbers.** Any claim in this run is read against the σ above.

## 5. The reading rule — fixed before scoring

Primary statistic per task: **Δ = r_adj(TARGETED) − r_adj(GENERIC)**, read against `3σ_Δ`
from §4. Secondary: `r_within_adj` (same sign convention; its draw noise is ~0.03–0.11 and
it cannot decide anything on its own), and `X/Y` recovered with the historical promoted-table
`r_adj` as the third equation, reported **with its propagated band** and never as a point.

**Deciding tasks: beall2017 and dablander2025.** goldwert2026 and kim2024 are declared, in
advance, as **low-power** (3σ thresholds of 0.19 and 0.35): they can corroborate a sign but
cannot establish or refute one. Two of the four are all this environment offers.

**Prediction, stated before scoring.** I expect **Δ > 0 on both deciding tasks** — content
targeting is the most legible thing about a message design, and the targeted items are the
ones a respondent can answer straight from the text. I expect it to be larger on
dablander2025 than on beall2017. I have no directional expectation for goldwert2026,
because there submission_1 is a map I authored today with no train-split validation.

**Decision rule for `A_MULT` in the target entry:**

1. **Δ > +3σ on both deciding tasks** → content targeting is where the message-level skill
   is. `A_MULT` is not the entry's weak point; it stays at **0.40** and the binding
   constraint remains R20's mix bound, *not* a raise: the entry may not exceed the validated
   mix band on the strength of this result.
2. **Δ < −3σ on either deciding task** → the targeted cells are a worse place to bet than a
   generic ordering. `A_MULT` is cut to **0.20** on one such task and to **0.00** if both
   deciding tasks are negative.
3. **The two deciding tasks disagree in sign, both beyond 3σ** → same shape as X2/M5: a
   study-specific parameter, not a mechanism. `A_MULT` is cut to **0.20**, the value at
   which the A term is a perturbation of the message level rather than a quarter of the
   table, and the failure is recorded under R18's family.
4. **|Δ| < 3σ on both deciding tasks** → no measurement was obtained. `A_MULT` **stays at
   0.40 unchanged**; the entry records that the effect, if any, is smaller than 0.05 r_adj
   in the regime where it could be seen, which is itself a bound the entry did not have.

I will not re-tune `A_MULT` on a non-significant result, and I will not read `X/Y` as a
multiplier unless its band excludes `alpha/beta`.

**Nothing in this run is a gate candidate.** Both submissions on every task are measurement
arms of this question; the promoted state (`DESIGN.md` §9.5) stands as the arm's method
regardless of the outcome.

## APPENDUM A (written after the four AM-ISO pairs were scored, before any further submission)

The four pairs of §3 are scored and the reading rule of §5 has been applied; the result is
recorded in `REPORT.md` §2 and `DESIGN.md` §10. This appendum covers **only** the three
tasks §6 declined, and it changes nothing about how §5 is read.

**Why they are now submitted at k = 1.** The AM-ISO result is strong enough that
"allocate the message-level budget to the content-targeted cells" is being declared as a
gate candidate (**M10**). A gate candidate must be *the mechanism applied uniformly*, not
the mechanism applied where it is convenient, so the candidate set has to cover all seven
tasks. One scored call each is spent for **coverage only**.

**Their diagnostics are declared uninformative in advance and will not be read as evidence
for or against M10**, exactly as §6 said: `altenmueller2024` saturates `r_adj` at 1.000,
and `kerwer2025` / `orchinik2024` have returned `truth_half_reliability` <= 0.13 on every
draw either has ever produced. This declaration is made before the files are written.

**Where M10 fires, by the rule of §1:**

* `kerwer2025` — **fires.** The arms differ by which *statement* is appended to a research
  summary: a conflict-of-interest statement (conflict / no conflict), a lay or scientific
  publication-bias statement, a practical-relevance statement, and the summary format
  (plain-language vs scientific abstract). A COI or publication-bias statement is an
  explicit claim about the researchers' **integrity**; a practical-relevance statement is an
  explicit claim about their **benevolence**. So
  `M = {trust_integrity_videogames, trust_integrity_psychotherapy,
  trust_benevolence_videogames, trust_benevolence_psychotherapy}` and the four
  `trust_expertise_*` / `credibility_*` outcomes are the generic (inferential) class.
* `altenmueller2024` — **does not fire.** The arms differ only in the institute's
  *discipline*, and no item asks about discipline; every outcome (trust adjectives, policy
  support, information seeking, ideological similarity) is a downstream inference.
* `orchinik2024` — **does not fire.** The two arms are essays on the history and the
  institutions of science; the 25 outcomes are conditional judgements at five stated
  consensus levels. No arm states or enacts any of them.

Where M10 does not fire the candidate is the baseline prediction unchanged — the same
regime-conditional non-firing that M1w-v2 used, and the same byte-identical-resubmission
caveat (OPEN A8: a cross-run byte-identical file is not guaranteed an independent draw and
contributes no information).
